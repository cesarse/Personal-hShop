# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Personal hShop is a Mac app made by César Seragiotto. It collects no personal data.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect or share any personal information. The little it saves stays on your Mac (see below).
- It never connects to the internet or to any online service. Its sandbox does not even allow outgoing connections.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does.
- It keeps no record of which files are downloaded, or by which device.

## What stays on your Mac

The app itself saves three settings, on your Mac only, inside its own sandbox:

- the server port;
- whether the page opens automatically when the server starts;
- a bookmark to the folder you chose, so the app can read it again the next time it runs.

macOS may also keep ordinary window details there, such as where the Settings window was. None of this leaves your Mac. Game titles read from your files are kept in memory while the app runs, and are gone when it quits.

## Your files and your network

- The app only reads the folder you choose. It never changes, moves or deletes your files.
- While the server is running, any device that can reach your Mac over the network, normally the devices on your local network, can open the page and download the files listed on it. The page and the files are sent over plain HTTP, without encryption, and the page shows the names of your games and your Mac's local address. The server stops when you stop it or quit the app, and never starts on its own. Start it only on networks you trust.

## Keeping and deleting your data

The settings above are kept until you delete them. Nothing is kept anywhere else and the developer receives no data, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete.

To stop the app reading your folder, choose another folder in Settings, or delete the app's settings as described below.

To delete the app's settings, quit the app, choose **Go › Go to Folder…** in the Finder, enter `~/Library/Containers` and move the folder named "Personal hShop" to the Trash. Its real name is `com.github.cesarse.Personal-hShop`. If macOS asks for permission, allow it.

Moving the app itself to the Trash does not delete this folder.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen in macOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports for this app. Apple handles those under its own privacy policy.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
